# Credits

## Photographs

The two background photographs used by the app window (a close-up of ferns, and a close-up of
leaves, in `assets/hero.jpg` and its history) were downloaded from the free photos on
[Magnific](https://www.magnific.com/free-photos-vectors/nature-wallpaper-green/2) (formerly
Freepik) and are used under Magnific's free licence, which requires crediting the author and
Magnific. Photos by their respective authors, via Magnific.

If you reuse this project, check the licence of those photographs, or replace them: run
`python3 scripts/make-hero.py` to regenerate a licence-free background, or
`python3 scripts/set-hero.py your-photo.jpg` to use your own.

The landing page in `docs/` does not use these photographs.

## Software

pdfsqueeze drives these programs, which are bundled in the release packages:
